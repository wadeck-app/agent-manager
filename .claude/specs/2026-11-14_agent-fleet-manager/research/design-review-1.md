# Design review 1 -- independent read of architecture, decisions, threat model and plan

Date: 2026-11-14. Independent read-only review by a fresh agent (no session context), asked to check the design against the real w-warp/herdr/w-agent-wake/w-command-log scripts, the sibling code and app-core constraints.
Status: items marked [V] were read or counted by the reviewer; the lead has NOT re-read them. UNVERIFIED = reviewer inference. Dispositions refer to decisions 29-41 in `_index.md`.

Overall verdict (reviewer): well researched and honest about unknowns; weak points are spawn volume, an unauthenticated reply route, the upload route and stale cross-references.
