## Conclusion

The best reuse is `app-module-flow` as the skeleton of the new app-module and its log-stream UI as the design of the log viewer. agent-fleet has no transcript parser, no reply box, no image handling, no sub-agent tracking and no collapsed tool-call view; `parse-jsonl.js` at the root is a 20-line throwaway reading `obj.role`, not the real transcript shape. Its orchestrator/worker/web-backend are specific to flow (headless workers it launches) and are not candidates; [V] `README.md:28-44` confirms it runs flows and does not observe user-started sessions.

## Hooks

`ClaudeHookTranslator.ts` only generates per-run PreToolUse/PostToolUse settings for flow steps; not relevant to global hooks. No Notification, Stop or SessionStart handling anywhere in the repo.

## Not found in agent-fleet

Reply box, image handling, desktop notifications (only `Toast.tsx`, grep only), sub-agent tracking, transcript viewer with collapsed tool calls. The "intervention" UI (`web-frontend/src/app/pages/interventions/InterventionDetailPage.tsx`, orchestrator `core/InterventionManager.ts`) is approve/reject plus a comment for a flow worker's request: tied to flow tasks, not reusable.

## Do not take

`orchestrator`, `worker`, `web-backend` services and storage (`FileBasedStorage`), `framework/` (large design system), lego/forms pages: all flow-specific.
